Who We Are

Bastel Pvt Ltd — Your Trusted Partner

Bastel (Pvt) Ltd is a fully registered Sri Lankan company engaged in the import, export, and trading of all types of legal goods. We provide comprehensive customs house agency and clearing services, freight handling, cargo documentation, fumigation coordination, and end-to-end logistics management.

Our expert team manages the full trade cycle: from initial quotation and freight booking through customs clearance, transport, fumigation, and final documentation — ensuring every shipment reaches its destination seamlessly.
